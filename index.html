How apps connect

Each app runs in a frame on this page. The desk speaks the same guest protocol as Discobot's rack, so any app that can be a Discobot guest instrument plugs in: it follows the desk's tempo and start/stop and sends its sound into its channel strip instead of its own speakers. Apps that don't speak it still load, but their sound can't reach the console. For those, add a Live input channel and route the sound in from your audio interface.
